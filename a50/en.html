FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the battery last on a full charge?

Runtime depends on the workload. Light dusting at lower load runs considerably longer; continuous full-power turbo output lasts around 15–25 minutes. The 4-LED indicator shows remaining charge at any time.

Can it really replace canned air for my PC?

Yes — with 90,000 RPM and 850 g of thrust, the A50 blows harder than a can of compressed air, and unlike canned air it never runs out, produces no freezing propellant, and creates zero waste.

Can I use it while it's charging?

We recommend using it on battery power. The USB-C port supports both input and output, so you can also use the A50 as a power bank for your phone in a pinch.

Is it safe around children or pets?

The hexagon intake guard prevents fingers and hair from reaching the impeller. As with any high-speed fan, keep it out of reach of small children and supervise use around pets.
